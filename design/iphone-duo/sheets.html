What this canvas decides (approved in Phase 5, decisions 13–22)

System placement, as measured in Phase 1. O and SV: the sheet spans the display 8 pt from each edge, with ✕ (cancellationAction) and ✓ (confirmationAction) at the top of the side bar. IL, IP and iPad: centered form sheet, 653 pt (580 pt on iPad), horizontal ✕ / ✓. FB: the system moves the sheet onto the left page. FT: below the division. With the keyboard up the sheet grows to the top; the frames record how much of the form stays visible.

F18, New Game. The preview board no longer fills the sheet. Sheets ≥ 600 pt wide put a capped board beside the form; narrower ones cap it at 40 % of the visible sheet, so the FEN field, its error and the offline note stay on screen with the keyboard up.

F19, offline. The "needs a connection" note moves to the top of the form, next to the disabled ✓, instead of below the visible area.

F20, comment editor. Solid background (secondarySystemBackground), so the board no longer shows through. The medium detent still covers the lower board on O while editing (F29, accepted).

Files picker and share sheet are system UI, drawn for placement only.
