What this canvas decides (for review)

Detail column (decision 12). On regular width these screens fill the detail column of the tab's split view: 526 pt on IL, the right page on FB, 368 pt on IP, 833 pt on iPad (content kept to 672 pt). The sidebar keeps the home list with the collection selected, so the hierarchy reads left to right. Compact keeps today's stack. Swipe actions, context menus and edit mode are unchanged.

Overflow (F16). The collection and game-collection ⋯ menus become ToolbarOverflowMenu. + (Add Games) keeps its own symbol and menu.

Search scopes (F23). .searchScopes(…, activation: .onSearchPresentation): the scope bar shows as soon as search is active, with or without a query, so "Flagged" can be picked and left without typing. S07c is drawn as the redefined "scope applied with a query".

Edit mode (F30). Select All / Deselect All becomes a titled toolbar item with a symbol, next to Done (prominent), instead of a text button over the list. The bulk items stay one .bottomBar group (Move, Copy, Export, Delete): symbols at the bottom of the side bar on O, SV, IL and FB (the tab bar is hidden in edit mode, as today), a titled capsule on IP, FT and iPad. Delete is red and last.

Dialogs and sheets. Delete confirmations anchor to the item that asked: the overflow item, the Delete bulk item, or the row (F15). The destination sheet is fitted to its rows (side ✕ / ✓ on compact, centered on regular, one page on FB). The share sheet is the system activity view: a sheet on compact, a popover from Export on regular.
