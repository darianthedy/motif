Motif · iPhone Duo · Shell and homes

What this canvas decides (for review)

Shell (decision 4). TabView with the system tab bar; each tab holds one NavigationSplitView. Compact (O, SV) collapses it to today's stack. Regular: the home list is the sidebar column and the selected collection is the detail (the collection screens themselves are on the Lists canvas). Pushing a board screen in the detail hides the sidebar, as the board canvases show. One container at every size, so folding keeps the path and selection (§6.15). Two columns, not three: at 867 pt a third column would leave the board screens under 300 pt.

Tab bar. Bottom of the side bar on O, SV, IL and FB; bottom on IP and FT; top on iPad. Symbols only in the side bar is system behavior (F31); the titles stay for VoiceOver and the bottom bar. .sidebarAdaptable is drawn once (S04a·IL) for comparison: the tab sidebar takes 200 pt and squeezes the detail to 365 pt for two tabs, so it is not chosen.

Toolbar (F32). The account avatar becomes a real toolbar item titled "Account", next to + (Import Puzzles / New Collection). In the side bar it sits at the top; on IP at the top of the sidebar column. On regular width the selected collection's ⋯ joins them.

FB. The split column boundary falls on the fold: sidebar on the left page, detail on the right. FT shows the IP layout: lists are scrolling content, which may cross the fold; alerts and sheets sit below it.

Banners (S17, F25, F26). Persistent status (Offline, Offline · N waiting) is a small pill trailing the large title. Transient banners (server error, dismissible error, toast) are a glass capsule at the bottom of the content, at most 480 pt wide, clear of the side bar and the tab bar, never over a board. The same rule is applied on the Analysis and Session canvases.

Sign in and launch are a centered column at most 480 pt wide on regular width. With the keyboard up, the logo and tagline collapse to the title so Email, Password and Sign In sit above the keyboard (F21 is fixed in code already). On FB with the keyboard, the column moves to the right page.

Account sheet. On compact it has a single toolbar item (Close), so it opts out of the vertical bar with toolbarVerticalBehavior and keeps ✕ in its own navigation bar. The reset confirmation anchors to the Reset row inside the sheet (F15: it showed outside the sheet on IL); the toast and the error banner sit at the bottom of the sheet (capture issue for S03j).

Dialogs. Rename / New Collection alerts are centered (on FB, on the left page with its column); delete confirmations anchor to the row that asked on regular width and are action sheets on compact.

§6 check

  1. System containers: TabView, NavigationSplitView, List, toolbar, ContentUnavailableView, alert, confirmationDialog, sheet.
  2. Size classes, not devices: the split collapses by itself.
  3. Same app in every pose: same tabs, items and rows.
  4. Reserved regions: nothing interactive under the camera; on FB the column boundary sits on the division.
  5. Bars: every item has a title and a symbol, including the avatar.
  6. Hit targets: rows ≥ 52 pt; toolbar items 44 pt.
  7. Readable width: sign-in, launch and detail content ≤ 672 pt; banners ≤ 480 pt (F13, F22, F26).
  8. Sheets: Account centered on regular, full width on compact.
  9. Dialogs: driven from state, anchored to their row.
  10. Typography: AX5 frames; rows grow, titles wrap.
  11. Color: secondary text uses secondaryLabel (F35 measured in D10).
  12. Glass on bars, tab bar, banners and menus only.
  13. Motion: system.
  14. VoiceOver: sidebar column, then detail, then toolbar.
  15. Continuity: one container, path and selection survive folding.
  16. Folding: small adjustments only.
